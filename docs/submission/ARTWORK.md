# Artwork and fonts

The room browser uses generated illustration sheets for original stories, fictional creative worlds and sample collaborators, and three authored fan-story examples. Night City uses anime cel shading; the female-Draco Hogwarts alternate universe has both anime and film-style visual interpretations; Blade Runner uses cinematic photographic noir. The featured original moon-bus story retains its gouache style.

The fan stories are unofficial interpretations with authored captions and fictional collaborators. They use newly generated artwork, rather than official franchise images or television scenes. The user supplied six fictional female-Draco references; these guide character design and the two rendering styles. Draco is a girl within the original Hogwarts storyline, replacing the male Draco. Both styles depict the same six prepared scenes and preserve the chosen branch and credits. The earlier adult-reunion scene studies remain available for older saved work, outside featured discovery. No release prediction or real fandom activity is implied.

The app loads their compressed WebP versions from [public/art](../../public/art). Uploaded desk images and user drawings stay in browser-local state until explicitly selected for a local publication/export.

The `/world-demo` Chapter 2 screen also plays a user-supplied, pre-generated KlingAI clip under **Prepared motion example**. Its [compressed MP4](../../public/world-demo/after-hours-kling-demo.mp4), [poster](../../public/world-demo/after-hours-kling-poster.webp) and [scene captions](../../public/world-demo/after-hours-kling-captions.vtt) are runtime presentation assets. The original video and processing record stay in the private local archive. The provider-specific generation button has been replaced; playback makes no provider request. Storyboard videos are rendered locally from the actual selected illustrations and captions.

The creator-review companion reuses two original imagegen continuation illustrations as [reunion](../../public/creator-demo/reunion.webp) and [neighborhood](../../public/creator-demo/neighborhood.webp) WebPs. Their original PNGs, exact prompts, source hashes and processing records remain in the private archive. Native origin metadata links each compressed image to its source hash. The images are genuine prior outputs; their use in a fictional AI creator’s activity record is explicitly prepared demonstration material.

Inter is self-hosted in [public/fonts](../../public/fonts) under the [SIL Open Font License](../../public/fonts/OFL.txt). That license remains with the font in submission copies.

The selected [Round 4 concept gallery](process/round-4/README.md) contains fourteen images generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. The public copies use lossless WebP at the original dimensions; decoded pixels match the selected PNG revisions. The [selection record](process/round-4/selection.json) preserves their source hashes and selected revision numbers. All depicted people, event photography, artwork, activity and economics are illustrative. Original PNGs, earlier revisions and full generation records remain in the private archive. These historical documentation images are separate from runtime artwork and the actual boat-demo feedback.
